Our year of festivals

The festivals we usually celebrate — exact dates are announced each year.

  1. January – February

    Saraswati Puja

    Worship of the goddess of learning, music and the arts.

  2. March

    Holi

    The festival of colours and the arrival of spring.

  3. Summer

    Summer picnic & BBQ

    A relaxed day out for families and friends.

  4. Summer

    Cambridge Mela

    Joining the city's celebration of South Asian culture.

  5. September – October

    Durga Puja

    Our flagship festival — rituals, cultural programmes and community bhog.

  6. October

    Bijoya Sammilani

    Get-together after Durga Puja to exchange greetings and sweets.

  7. October – November

    Kali Puja & Diwali

    The festival of lights, with puja, fireworks and food.
